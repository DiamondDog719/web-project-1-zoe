How to Use Moves in Pokémon Pokopia

Moves are abilities Ditto (the player) can use in Pokémon Pokopia. They are one of the most important game mechanics as they are important for getting around, collecting materials, and interacting with the world.

As of the time of writing, there are 11 major moves in the game. They are:

  • Water Gun
  • Leafage
  • Rock Smash
  • Rototiller
  • Cut
  • Camouflage
  • Rollout
  • Magnet Rise
  • Surf
  • Dive
  • Glide

Jump, Strength, and Suck up Liquid are also considered moves but aren't complex enough to need much description. Waterfall is another move that is included in the page for Surf.

PP Points

Moves in Pokémon Pokopia use PP (Power Points). A curved bar in your circular move menu shows how much you have left. You need enough PP to use moves, so make sure to use them wisely.

PP can be restored by eating food or resting in a Pokémon Centre. Some foods (such as cooked dishes) will restore more PP than others.
